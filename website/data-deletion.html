Data Deletion Request

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

In accordance with Google Play and Apple App Store user data policies, Bookmarkt provides users with direct and simple ways to delete their accounts and associated data.

Method 1: In-App Self-Service Deletion (Immediate)

You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the Bookmarkt mobile application:

  1. Open the Bookmarkt app.
  2. Navigate to Settings (gear icon).
  3. Tap on Your data.
  4. Scroll down and select Delete account and data.
  5. Confirm your decision.

Once confirmed, your account credentials, reading notes, quotes, book tracking, and session histories are immediately and permanently removed from our primary database.

Method 2: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, you can request that our support team delete your account and data:

Send an email to support@bookmarkt.io with the subject line "Account and Data Deletion Request" from the email address associated with your Bookmarkt account.

Our team will verify ownership and process the complete deletion of your account and associated records within 30 days.

Types of Data Deleted

  • User profile and authentication records (email address, user ID).
  • All logged books, reading sessions, timers, entries, quotes, and reflections.
  • All cached AI comprehension scores, companion conversation history, and cue card stats.
